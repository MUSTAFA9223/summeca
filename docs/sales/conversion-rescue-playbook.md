# SUMMECA — 48-Hour Service Sales Playbook

This playbook is for fast, direct sales of SUMMECA's three service offers without requiring an upfront payment.

## Offer ladder

### 1) Primary: 24-Hour Conversion Rescue
- $250 — Ready-to-Implement Pack
- $500 — Implementation Pack
- Best fit: local service businesses, B2B services, consultants, agencies, coaches, and ecommerce businesses with a live page that already receives traffic.
- Sales rule: show a small private preview first, then request payment before final editable handoff or publishing.

### 2) Plan B: Ecommerce Product Page Rescue
- $149 — 5 products
- $399 — 20 products
- Best fit: Shopify and other ecommerce stores with weak product copy.
- Sales rule: show a partial rewrite of one real product as proof before payment.

### 3) Plan C: Local Visibility Quick Pack
- $99 — one business profile content pack
- Best fit: local businesses with an active Google Business Profile but weak descriptions, unanswered reviews, weak Q&A, or sparse posts.
- Sales rule: show two sample review responses or one improved business-description paragraph before payment.

## Prospect qualification — 60 seconds maximum

Before contacting a business, confirm at least one visible, factual problem.

For Conversion Rescue, look for:
- vague or generic hero headline
- primary CTA not obvious
- important CTA below the fold on mobile
- unclear offer or audience
- long blocks of copy with no scannable benefits
- weak trust or proof placement
- confusing form or booking flow

For Ecommerce Rescue, look for:
- generic product titles
- feature-only descriptions with no customer benefit
- duplicated manufacturer copy
- weak bullets
- missing or unclear CTA context
- weak meta description or missing image-alt opportunities

For Local Visibility Pack, look for:
- weak business description
- sparse service descriptions
- many reviews with no owner response
- repetitive or low-quality review replies
- few Google posts
- obvious customer questions not answered in profile content

Never invent a problem. If the issue cannot be justified from what is visible, skip the prospect.

## AI prompt — Conversion Rescue

Use this only after reviewing the page yourself and copying factual business information from the page.

```text
Act as a conversion-rate optimization specialist.

Analyze the page and business information below.

Business:
[FACTUAL BUSINESS INFORMATION ONLY]

Page content:
[PASTE RELEVANT CONTENT]

Primary conversion goal:
[BOOK / REQUEST QUOTE / BUY / CONTACT]

Identify only the three most important conversion problems that can be justified from the supplied page content.

Then produce:
1. Hero headline
2. Subheadline
3. Primary CTA
4. Three benefit bullets
5. Recommended section order
6. Five factual FAQ questions the business should answer
7. Three concise lead follow-up messages

Rules:
- Do not invent statistics.
- Do not invent testimonials or customer counts.
- Do not invent guarantees or certifications.
- Do not claim the business is #1 or “best” without evidence.
- Preserve factual pricing, restrictions, and service areas exactly.
- Keep copy specific, concise, and easy to scan on mobile.
```

## Delivery checklist — $250 pack

## Delivery checklist — $500 implementation pack

Everything in the $250 pack, plus:
- confirm platform is supported before accepting the package
- work only with the minimum access required
- create a backup or confirm an existing rollback path before editing production
- implement only the approved scope
- test desktop and mobile
- test CTA links and forms
- send preview before final publish where possible
- do not change unrelated site functionality

## Payment and handoff rule

The sequence is:
1. Prospect shares URL and goal.
2. SUMMECA prepares a limited private preview.
3. Prospect approves the direction and exact scope.
4. SUMMECA sends the payment request/invoice.
5. After confirmed payment, SUMMECA sends editable final files or publishes the approved implementation.

Do not send the complete editable work before payment.
Do not call an invoice “paid” until payment is actually confirmed.

## Metrics to track during the 48-hour sprint

Track:
- prospects reviewed
- qualified prospects
- first messages sent
- replies
- positive replies
- sample/teardown requests
- price questions
- approved scopes
- invoices/payment requests sent
- confirmed payments
- average time to create each sample

Interpretation:
- no replies: improve targeting or first message
- replies but no price questions: improve the offer or proof
- price questions but no closes: improve trust, scope clarity, or pricing fit
- approvals but no payment: simplify payment process and confirm scope clearly

## Non-negotiable claims policy

Never promise a specific sales result, ranking, conversion increase, revenue increase, or lead count.
Never fabricate portfolio results, client names, testimonials, screenshots, certifications, or metrics.
Sell the quality of the actual preview and the speed/clarity of the scope.
